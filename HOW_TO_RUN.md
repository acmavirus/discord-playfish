Welcome to the Virtual Farmer Wiki!

The gameplay of the bot is very similiar to Virtual Fisher but instead of fishing the design of the game is about farming. Biome
Biomes are locations which you can farm in. You farm different crop using different hoes in each biome. When you unlock a biome the hoes specialized for this biome are also unlocked along with a few other features including upgrades, biome-specific hoes. All buffs are independent of biome. Each biome increase your wood multiplier, better biome has better wood multiplier.

Clan
A clan is a social group of players who can work together towards shared goals, such as leveling up, acquiring resources, and competing in leaderboards.

In Virtual Farmer, you can create your own clan when you reached level 10 and above. But you can join a clan at any level.

Every trip contribute to 1 XP to clan. Other actions also contribute XP to your clan.
